Frequently Asked Questions

Below are answers to some commonly asked questions about our real estate services in Riyadh. If you need further details, feel free to reach out and we'll be happy to assist.

  • What is the process for buying a property through Rimak United?

    Our process is simple: contact us to discuss your requirements, we then shortlist suitable properties for you to visit, and once you choose your preferred option, we handle all the paperwork and procedures to ensure a smooth and legally compliant transaction.

  • Can I schedule a property viewing before committing?

    Absolutely. We encourage all clients to visit and inspect properties before making any decisions. Simply contact us and we will arrange a convenient viewing time at no obligation.

    Our property management service covers tenant sourcing, rent collection, maintenance coordination, periodic inspections, and regular reporting to property owners — so you can enjoy returns without the day-to-day hassle.
